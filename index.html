The round, from kickoff to report

  1. Day 0

    Scope the rubric

    We agree with your domain lead on what "accurate, helpful and safe" means in your product. You approve it in your client portal.

  2. Day 1–2

    Calibrate

    Raters work through sample answers together until they apply your rubric the same way.

  3. Day 2–8

    Two blind reviews per answer

    Each answer is scored by two experts who can't see each other's work. Both write down their reasoning.

  4. Day 8–9

    Adjudicate

    Any disagreement, or any review that fails a quality check, goes to a senior reviewer, who makes the final call.

  5. Day 10

    Deliver

    A findings report (win rate, top failure types, worst examples with the experts' notes), the labeled data, and agreement statistics.

Ratings you can defend to an auditor

Every safeguard is built into our own review platform, and every result comes with the evidence behind it.

Verified credentials
Licenses and certifications are checked against the issuing body's public registry, skills are tested, and identity is confirmed on a live call.
Hidden control items
Answers with known ratings are mixed into every queue. Accuracy slips mean a pause and recalibration.
Work-integrity checks
Pasted or AI-written reviews, scripted input and impossible speeds are flagged, and a person reviews every flag.
Agreement statistics
Krippendorff's alpha for every criterion, so you know exactly how consistent the experts were.
Secure by default
Two-factor sign-in for staff, an access log of every view and export, and data deleted after delivery. Security details

Questions

Why human reviewers instead of an LLM judge?

LLM judges are useful for cheap screening, but they share blind spots with the models they grade. Where one wrong fact can hurt someone, you need a person who'd catch it. Many teams use our labels to check and tune their automated judges.

What do you need from us?

Prompts and your model's answers, as JSON or CSV. To compare two versions, send both answers for each prompt.

Can you handle patient data (PHI)?

Tell us before sending any. We'll arrange de-identification, or a BAA with HIPAA-eligible hosting, before any PHI is shared. See Security.

Who owns the results?

You do. All ratings, notes and reports are assigned to you on payment.

How fast can you start?

Usually within a week of agreeing the scope.
