The problem

Your in-house experts can't rate 2,000 outputs. Big labeling platforms aren't built for a team your size.

When your team ships a prompt change, a new model or a RAG update into a clinical, legal or security product, someone has to answer one question: did it get better or worse?

Automated LLM judges miss the domain errors that matter most: a wrong dosage, an outdated statute, a misread CVE. Your own clinicians and counsel are too expensive and too busy to grade thousands of answers. The large data platforms are built around foundation-model labs and their volumes.

We sit in between: a vetted network of domain experts, a rigorous review process, and a team that works at mid-market scale and speed.

How it works

Two raters, one adjudicator, zero guesswork

  1. Scope

    We agree on the rubric with your domain lead: what counts as accurate, helpful and safe in your product.

  2. Calibrate

    Raters work through sample items together until they apply your rubric the same way.

  3. Rate

    Each item is scored by two experts who can't see each other's work. Each one writes a justification for the rating.

  4. Adjudicate

    Any disagreement, or any rating that fails a quality check, goes to a senior reviewer.

  5. Deliver

    You get labeled data (JSONL), agreement statistics and a findings report you can act on.

Quality you can audit

How we keep the ratings honest

Hidden control items

Items with known answers are mixed into every queue and look identical to real work. Raters whose accuracy on them slips are paused and recalibrated.

Work-integrity checks

Our platform flags signs of copy-pasted or automated answers: large text dumps, inhuman typing patterns, implausibly fast submissions. A person reviews every flag.

Verified credentials

Before an SME touches your data, we check their license or certification against the issuing body's public registry and confirm their identity on a live call.

FAQ

Common questions

Why human raters instead of an LLM judge?

LLM judges are useful for broad, cheap screening, but they share blind spots with the models they grade. In domains where one wrong fact causes harm, you need a person who'd catch it. Many teams use our labels to check and tune their automated judges.

What data do you need from us?

Prompts and model outputs, usually as JSON or CSV. For comparisons, send two responses per prompt. They can come from two models, two prompts, or your model against a competitor's.

Can you handle patient data (PHI)?

Tell us before you send any. We'll scope de-identification, or a BAA with HIPAA-eligible hosting, before any PHI is shared. See Security.

Who owns the labels?

You do. All ratings, justifications and reports are assigned to you on payment.

How fast can you start?

A sprint usually starts within a week of scoping, depending on the domain and how many credentialed raters it needs.
