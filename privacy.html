Privacy

Last updated October 31, 2026

This website

This site has no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies or advertising. Our host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.

When you email us

If you contact us or apply to be a rater, we use what you send only to reply, evaluate your application and, if you join, manage your work with us. We don't sell or share it with anyone except service providers we need to run the business, such as email and payment providers.

Applicants and raters

To verify credentials, we look up the license or certification details you give us in public registries. Raters who join receive a separate notice explaining the work-pattern data our rating platform collects during tasks.

Retention and your choices

We delete applications that don't move forward within 12 months. You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing LucasMaillet2019@gmail.com.
