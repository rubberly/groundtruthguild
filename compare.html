The setup most teams end up with

Use an AI judge for volume, and experts to keep it honest. Automated judges are cheap enough to score every answer. Expert reviews on a few hundred answers tell you where the AI judge is wrong, and give you labels to tune it.

When the stakes are high (health, money, legal rights, security), have experts review what you ship.
