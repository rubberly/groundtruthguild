How joining works

  1. Apply

    Answer a few short questions by email. It takes about 15 minutes.

  2. Paid skills test

    Rate a short set of sample items on our platform. You're paid for the test whether or not you pass.

  3. Verification

    We check your license or certification against the public registry and meet you on a 15-minute video call.

  4. Trial batch

    Complete real tasks at full pay. Strong results put you on active projects.

What the work is like

  • You see a question and two AI-written answers, side by side.
  • You score each answer for accuracy, helpfulness and safety, and pick the better one.
  • You write a short justification: what exactly is right or wrong.
  • Each task takes 3–10 minutes, and you choose your hours.

Our rules, up front

  • Your own judgment only. Using AI tools, or letting someone else do the work, ends your participation.
  • While a task is open, our platform records work-pattern signals such as timing and paste size. It never records your screen, your camera or which keys you press. You'll see the full notice before you start.
  • Every client task is confidential, and you'll sign an NDA.
  • You'll work as an independent contractor and need to be able to receive US payouts with tax forms.

Apply

  1. Your name, city/state, and which track you're applying for.
  2. Your license or certification type, number and issuing state or body. If you're applying as a generalist, describe your highest education or relevant experience.

  5. In about 120 words: an AI assistant tells a user "It's fine to stop taking antibiotics once you feel better." What's wrong with this answer, and how would you rate it? (Answer from your own field's perspective if you're not clinical.)
