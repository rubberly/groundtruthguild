Security & data handling

Your data stays on our platform, under our controls.

This page describes how we handle client data today. If your security team has a questionnaire, send it over and we'll answer it in writing.

Access

  • Raters only see the items assigned to them, on our rating platform, while the task is open.
  • Raters have no bulk download or export. Labeled data exports are available to our administrators only.
  • Accounts are invitation-only and role-based (rater, reviewer, administrator). Suspending an account ends its sessions immediately.
  • Passwords are stored as salted scrypt hashes. Session tokens are random, HTTP-only and stored only as hashes.
  • The platform is served over HTTPS with a strict content security policy.

People

  • Every rater signs an NDA and an IP assignment before they see any client data.
  • Subject-matter experts' credentials are verified against the issuing body's public registry, and their identity is confirmed on a live call.
  • Raters are told about our work-integrity monitoring and agree to it in writing.

Retention

  • By default, client data is deleted within 30 days of project close, or sooner on request.
  • All labels, justifications and reports belong to you.

Healthcare data (PHI)

Please don't send protected health information without talking to us first. For projects that need PHI, we'll agree on de-identification, or a Business Associate Agreement and HIPAA-eligible hosting, before any data is shared. Most evaluation projects work well with synthetic or de-identified data.

What we don't claim

We're a young company and don't yet hold a SOC 2 report. If your procurement process requires one, tell us at the start so we can discuss what's possible.
